PROBLEMA (comum aos dois braços):
A nonprofit cooperative manages donated repair parts at eight workshops. There are about 45,000 individual parts and 60 volunteers. They use inexpensive Android phones to record arrivals, moves between workshops, setting a part aside for a repair, use of a part, and corrections. A workshop may have no internet connection for up to 72 hours. Different volunteers may act on or describe the same part before their messages reach one another. Their current spreadsheets lead to wasted trips, promises of parts that are no longer available, and corrections that erase another person's observations. Photos and the history of what people recorded matter. Only two generalist developers can maintain the system, recurring costs must stay small, and the cooperative must be able to export its data. Work must continue during disconnection; volunteers cannot be required to travel to a central office every day. What should change in the work and the software to materially reduce these problems? Investigate alternative designs, explain the decisions and trade-offs each changes, and support them with real software sources. Propose a small way to evaluate the alternatives before committing to one.
